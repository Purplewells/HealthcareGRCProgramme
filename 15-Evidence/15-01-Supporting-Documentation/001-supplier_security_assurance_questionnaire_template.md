# Supplier Security Assurance Questionnaire (Template)

**Organisation:** Westbridge Hospitals Trust (WHT)
**Document Type:** Template
**Owner:** Procurement Director
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

This is a blank template, not a completed record — it is the instrument that would be used to deliver the Trust-wide supplier security assurance process recommended in [../../04-Risk-Management/043-third_party_risks.md](../../04-Risk-Management/043-third_party_risks.md) REC-001, and referenced by the same gap identified independently in [../../03-Current-State-Assessment/022-caf_assessment.md](../../03-Current-State-Assessment/022-caf_assessment.md) (CAF A4), [../../03-Current-State-Assessment/023-ISO27001_assessment.md](../../03-Current-State-Assessment/023-ISO27001_assessment.md) (A.5.19–A.5.23), and [../../03-Current-State-Assessment/024-dsp_toolkit_review.md](../../03-Current-State-Assessment/024-dsp_toolkit_review.md) (Standard 10). It exists here as a process artefact — a form to be filled in — not as a record of any specific supplier engagement, consistent with the distinction drawn in [README](README.md) between templates and fabricated evidence.

## When to Use

At initial supplier onboarding, and at the annual re-assessment cycle referenced in [../../14-RoadMap/141-cyber_security_roadmap.md](../../14-RoadMap/141-cyber_security_roadmap.md) INIT-02. Priority for the first assessment cycle should follow [../../02-Asset-Management/025-supplier_register.xlsx](../../02-Asset-Management/025-supplier_register.xlsx) — suppliers with access to Restricted-classified data first.
